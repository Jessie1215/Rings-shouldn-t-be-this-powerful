Prologue

The World of Lenithia

Throughout history, there have always been rings imbued with extraordinary power — legendary artifacts known as Fabled Rings. These mystical treasures possessed unique abilities that transcended ordinary magic. But their fate took a dark turn during a devastating war that occurred long ago. Many of these powerful rings were lost to time, and some were destroyed amid the chaos.

Fabled Rings

The surviving rings, still brimming with immense power, were entrusted to select organizations and prestigious schools, each dedicated to preserving and controlling their ancient magic. Their locations are known only to a few, and their power is guarded from those who might misuse it.

Arcane Rings

Common yet potent, Arcane Rings channel elemental magic — fire, ice, wind, and earth. Each is enhanced by a specific stone: sapphire calls water, and others answer to other elements. Arcane Rings are tasked with protecting the Fabled Rings and keeping the elemental balance.

The world of Lenithia now faces a dire threat. Portals to other realms — once sealed by the Fabled Rings — are beginning to open, threatening to unleash chaos and destruction. It now falls to the bearers of both rings to harness their combined power, close the gateways, and keep the calamity from consuming everything.

In a hidden chamber beneath the prestigious academy of Athenia — rumored to be built atop the ruins of an older civilization — a young student named Jessie uncovers an Arcane Ring behind a secret door in the library. As he holds it, a faint, mysterious energy pulses through the band.

Jessie

“Woah — an Arcane Ring! I thought these were usually hidden somewhere in the school, but this doesn't look like a normal Arcane Ring.”

He examines the ring and the scroll it was found with. The scroll is inscribed with ancient symbols related to ice magic — skills and incantations for controlling ice. His excitement fades into concern.

Jessie, to himself

I don't even know how to use an Arcane Ring, and I definitely don't have any PriVo to activate it.

Jessie, to himself

Well — I do have some PriVo, but I still don't know how to use an Arcane Ring. Before any of that, I need to get out of here before someone finds me.

Jessie, to himself

I definitely don't want detention because of this. The Academy of Athenia doesn't really like misbehaving students.

Determined to understand more about the ring and its potential, Jessie resolves to study the scroll and seek guidance from someone who knows about Arcane Rings.

❅

After escaping the hidden chamber, Jessie returns to class, his mind racing from the discovery. When his professor asks where he's been, Jessie answers—

Jessie

“Sir, I went to the bathroom.”

He sits back down. His good friend Ramon leans over.

Ramon

“Dude — thirty minutes to go to the bathroom? What the hell were you doing in there?”

Jessie

“Dude, come on — a man's got to relieve himself somehow.”

Ramon

“Alright, you're not wrong. Are you busy later? I was thinking of coming to your house to do the project.”

Jessie

“What project? Maaan, I'm tired of this.”

Ramon

“I was gone for a week — family problem — and I have to catch up. I believe you're done with it, so you don't have to worry.”

Jessie

“Oh, that project — yeah, I'm done. I can help you, but I'm busy later, sorry man.”

Ramon

“No problem, man.”

The teacher notices the two of them talking and cuts in.

Professor

“You two — stop talking and listen. You should be lucky you got into this academy. We don't usually accept commoners.”

Jessie, to himself

Gosh — even the professors don't like commoners.

Ramon

“Yeah. No joke.”

❅

Distracted through the rest of class, Jessie can't stop thinking about the ring. Once classes end, he heads to a secluded spot behind the academy and unrolls the scroll — only to find it's written in a language he doesn't understand.

Jessie, frustrated

“If I'm really going to do this, I need a mentor.”

His thoughts turn to Sir Mular — a professor he's always admired despite his strict nature.

Jessie, to himself

I don't even know if Sir Mular would agree to be my mentor… but he's a good teacher, and I have to try.

With newfound determination, Jessie sets out to find Sir Mular after classes end, knowing the professor often stays late to practice magic. He knocks on the office door — no answer, which is odd, since Sir Mular is almost always there. Searching the campus, he eventually finds him alone in the courtyard, practicing his Arcane Ring magic.

Jessie, to himself

I have to try my best… look at him, he's doing it effortlessly. Damn.

As he walks toward Sir Mular, he narrowly dodges a stray water spear.

Sir Mular, smirking

“Well done, Jessie. You dodged that pretty well. What brings you here?”

Jessie, flustered

“Sir — will you be my mentor?”

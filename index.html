Prologue

The World of Lenithia

Throughout history, there have always been rings imbued with extraordinary power — legendary artifacts known as Fabled Rings. These mystical treasures possessed unique abilities that transcended ordinary magic. But their fate took a dark turn during a devastating war that occurred long ago. Many of these powerful rings were lost to time, and some were destroyed amid the chaos.

Fabled Rings

The surviving rings, still brimming with immense power, were entrusted to select organizations and prestigious schools, each dedicated to preserving and controlling their ancient magic. Their locations are known only to a few, and their power is guarded from those who might misuse it.

Arcane Rings

Common yet potent, Arcane Rings channel elemental magic — fire, ice, wind, and earth. Each is enhanced by a specific stone: sapphire calls water, and others answer to other elements. Arcane Rings are tasked with protecting the Fabled Rings and keeping the elemental balance.

The world of Lenithia now faces a dire threat. Portals to other realms — once sealed by the Fabled Rings — are beginning to open, threatening to unleash chaos and destruction. It now falls to the bearers of both rings to harness their combined power, close the gateways, and keep the calamity from consuming everything.

Chapter One

Humble Beginnings

In a hidden chamber beneath the prestigious academy of Athenia — rumored to be built atop the ruins of an older civilization — a young student named Jessie uncovers an Arcane Ring behind a secret door in the library. As he holds it, a faint, mysterious energy pulses through the band.

Jessie

“Woah — an Arcane Ring! I thought these were usually hidden somewhere in the school, but this doesn't look like a normal Arcane Ring.”

He examines the ring and the scroll it was found with. The scroll is inscribed with ancient symbols related to ice magic — skills and incantations for controlling ice. His excitement fades into concern.

Jessie, to himself

I don't even know how to use an Arcane Ring, and I definitely don't have any PriVo to activate it.

Jessie, to himself

Well — I do have some PriVo, but I still don't know how to use an Arcane Ring. Before any of that, I need to get out of here before someone finds me.

Jessie, to himself

I definitely don't want detention because of this. The Academy of Athenia doesn't really like misbehaving students.

Determined to understand more about the ring and its potential, Jessie resolves to study the scroll and seek guidance from someone who knows about Arcane Rings.

❅

After escaping the hidden chamber, Jessie returns to class, his mind racing from the discovery. When his professor asks where he's been, Jessie answers—

Jessie

“Sir, I went to the bathroom.”

He sits back down. His good friend Ramon leans over.

Ramon

“Dude — thirty minutes to go to the bathroom? What the hell were you doing in there?”

Jessie

“Dude, come on — a man's got to relieve himself somehow.”

Ramon

“Alright, you're not wrong. Are you busy later? I was thinking of coming to your house to do the project.”

Jessie

“What project? Maaan, I'm tired of this.”

Ramon

“I was gone for a week — family problem — and I have to catch up. I believe you're done with it, so you don't have to worry.”

Jessie

“Oh, that project — yeah, I'm done. I can help you, but I'm busy later, sorry man.”

Ramon

“No problem, man.”

The teacher notices the two of them talking and cuts in.

Professor

“You two — stop talking and listen. You should be lucky you got into this academy. We don't usually accept commoners.”

Jessie, to himself

Gosh — even the professors don't like commoners.

Ramon

“Yeah. No joke.”

❅

Distracted through the rest of class, Jessie can't stop thinking about the ring. Once classes end, he heads to a secluded spot behind the academy and unrolls the scroll — only to find it's written in a language he doesn't understand.

Jessie, frustrated

“If I'm really going to do this, I need a mentor.”

His thoughts turn to Sir Mular — a professor he's always admired despite his strict nature.

Jessie, to himself

I don't even know if Sir Mular would agree to be my mentor… but he's a good teacher, and I have to try.

With newfound determination, Jessie sets out to find Sir Mular after classes end, knowing the professor often stays late to practice magic. He knocks on the office door — no answer, which is odd, since Sir Mular is almost always there. Searching the campus, he eventually finds him alone in the courtyard, practicing his Arcane Ring magic.

Jessie, to himself

I have to try my best… look at him, he's doing it effortlessly. Damn.

As he walks toward Sir Mular, he narrowly dodges a stray water spear.

Sir Mular, smirking

“Well done, Jessie. You dodged that pretty well. What brings you here?”

Jessie, flustered

“Sir — will you be my mentor?”

Jessie, flustered

“Sir — will you be my mentor?”

Sir Mular

“Mentor? For what?”

Jessie

“For my Arcane Ring.”

Sir Mular

“Your Arcane Ring? How old are you again?”

Jessie

“I'm fifteen, sir. And yes, I have an Arcane Ring — I hope you could teach me how to use my mana.”

Sir Mular, shaking his head

“I'm not going to be your mentor unless you tell me everything.”

Jessie, with a sigh

“I found it in a hidden room in the academy library.”

Sir Mular, turning serious

“A hidden room, you say? In the library?”

Jessie

“Yes, I found it in a hidden room in the library, sir.”

Sir Mular, narrowing his eyes

“The school library is guarded by powerful magic. That's why some rooms are sealed. Only Sir Isaac and a few professors are supposed to have access.”

Jessie

“Well, technically I didn't know that room was there. I really didn't want to go back to class, so I went to the library instead.”

Sir Mular

“If you want me to be your mentor, you have to finish all your assignments — by tomorrow.”

Jessie, groaning

“Fine. If that's what it takes!”

Sir Mular

“We have a deal. Now — you know you still aren't allowed to have an Arcane Ring. I'll keep it as a little secret until exams.”

Jessie

“Sir, come on — I still want to join the exams, how about—”

❅

That night, Jessie searches frantically for the assignments he's let pile up, determined to prove himself. He's never been fond of literature, but he pushes through, working late into the night, nearly finishing by morning. Exhausted, he wakes up running late, packs his things, showers in a hurry, and rushes out the door without breakfast.

At school, dread sets in when he remembers — he left the homework at home. Checking his schedule, he sees Sir Mular's class falls right after the break, and spends the morning unable to focus.

When break finally comes, Jessie sprints home, narrowly avoiding the guards, grabs his assignments, and remembers too late that students aren't allowed to leave campus during school hours. He sneaks back in, slipping past the guards a second time, and arrives at Sir Mular's class just as it begins — assignments in hand, relieved but nervous.

Sir Mular inspects the papers with a raised eyebrow, says nothing for a long, tense moment, then finally nods.

Sir Mular

“You've proven your dedication, Jessie. But let me be clear — training to master an Arcane Ring isn't just about using magic. It's about discipline, focus, and understanding the responsibility you hold.”

Jessie, enthusiastically

“I'm ready, sir.”

Sir Mular, firm

“Good. Your first lesson begins tonight. Meet me at the edge of the old forest after dusk. And bring the ring.”

❅

Night falls. Jessie sneaks out to the edge of the forest, where Sir Mular waits beneath the shadow of towering trees, the air tense, as if the forest itself is watching. Sir Mular guides him into a small clearing.

Sir Mular

“Your Arcane Ring channels ice magic, but it's not just about summoning ice. It's about becoming one with the element — cold, unyielding, relentless. Show me what you've learned so far.”

Jessie hesitates, unsure if he can control the ring, then slides it onto his finger and closes his eyes, recalling the incantations from the scroll. The air around him chills. Frost gathers on his fingertips, and an icicle takes shape — sharp, glowing under the moonlight.

Sir Mular, nodding

“Not bad. But to master the ring, you must be able to control it in any situation — even in fear.”

Without warning, Sir Mular summons a water spear of his own and hurls it toward Jessie, who barely dodges in time.

Sir Mular

“Stay focused!”

A water ball follows. Jessie blocks it with a small, hastily formed ice shield.

Jessie, a little shaken

“Sir Mular, I swear, if you hit me I won't become your student — I'm kidding, I'm kidding!”

Sir Mular

“Won't become my student, huh? Take this and be grateful, kid — I'm not the one who begged for a mentor.”

Jessie, genuinely scared now

“Sir Mular, I was kidding—”

Over the next few hours, Sir Mular pushes Jessie to his limits — reflexes, endurance, concentration — until Jessie can't go on any longer.

Sir Mular

“You did well, Jessie. But remember — this is only the beginning. True mastery comes with time. And sacrifice.”

❅

Days pass, and the training intensifies. Each session tests Jessie further, but bit by bit his control over the Arcane Ring grows — as though the ring itself recognizes his determination.

Sir Mular, to himself

He's picked up the basics — ice shards, ice walls — faster than I expected for an ungrateful kid. His determination is worth mentioning. But if he wants to master that ring, he's going to need more than just determination.

One evening, after a particularly exhausting day, Jessie sits alone studying the scroll once more. His tired eyes catch a small, faded inscription along the edge he hadn't noticed before. It reads:

“The Fabled Rings — guardians of realms, keepers of balance.”

A chill runs down his spine. Were the Arcane Rings truly meant only to protect the Fabled Rings — or did they hold secrets of their own?

Determined to find answers, Jessie sneaks past the guards that night and slips into the academy's restricted library — the air thick with dust and secrets, shelves lined with tomes that hold centuries of knowledge. His fingers brush a thick, worn book: The Lost Artifacts of the Realms.

Flipping through it, he reads of the Fabled Rings' creation and the devastating war that scattered them across the world. His heart pounds when he comes across a passage that makes him pause:

“In times of darkness, the Fabled Rings shall awaken, each chosen wielder entrusted with protecting the balance. But beware — for power, once tasted, may consume even the noblest of souls.”

Jessie, to himself

Something isn't right. I'm not sure how, but this has to do with the Fabled Rings.

A shiver of awareness interrupts his thoughts — someone else is in the library.

A Cold Voice

“Interesting, isn't it?”

Jessie whirls around to find a tall figure cloaked in shadow, a cruel smirk twisting his lips — an unsettling calm about him, like a storm held just barely in check.

Feeling the danger, Jessie raises his hand on instinct, channeling his mana into the ring. In a flash of icy light, a sword of pure ice takes shape in his grip, its edges gleaming. The weapon feels right — the chill sinking into his bones, making him feel stronger, steadier.

The Stranger

“An ice sword? Bold choice. It's not easily made — some say it's driven by emotion. Are you afraid of me? Let's see how well you handle it.”

A bolt of lightning streaks toward Jessie. He brings up the ice sword just in time, absorbing part of the blast, his hand left tingling. The stranger seems unfazed — testing him, clearly someone who's carried an Arcane Ring far longer than Jessie has.

Pushing past his nerves, Jessie tightens his grip and lunges. The stranger sidesteps with ease, a flicker of amusement in his eyes, and answers with a quick, crackling jab of lightning. Jessie goes down — but he gets back up. His determination doesn't falter.

The Stranger

“Power like that doesn't come without consequences. Remember — your ring wasn't meant to be.”

Before Jessie can respond, the figure fades into the shadows, his laughter lingering like a haunting echo.

Jessie, shouting after him

“What do you mean by 'my ring wasn't meant to be'?!”

He tries to stand and can't — not with his injuries. He looks down at the icy blade still in his hand, realizing he's just unlocked a new level of control over it. With each challenge, it's becoming clearer that this weapon will be part of his journey — a symbol of his bond with the ring. But the questions are only multiplying.

Footsteps and muffled voices echo from outside — the guards have heard the commotion. Heart pounding, Jessie extinguishes the ice sword and crawls behind a towering shelf, holding his breath as their footsteps grow louder. They sweep the room, eyes passing over ancient tomes and dim corners, and find nothing. After a tense few moments, they leave, locking the door behind them.

Jessie waits until the silence fully returns, then slips out of the library, his injuries making it hard to walk straight.

❅

Restless, Jessie spends the rest of the night turning over the stranger's warning and the weight of the Fabled Rings' history. His grip on his ice has given him confidence — but he knows the man in the library was stronger than him, and that he needs to push his training further.

The next day, Jessie arrives at the training grounds earlier than usual. Sir Mular notices the intensity in his gaze — and the bandages on his burnt arms.

Sir Mular

“Restless night?”

Jessie, choosing his words carefully

“I… learned some things about the Fabled Rings. About the darkness that comes with wielding them.”

Sir Mular

“Power often comes at a price, Jessie. The Fabled Rings were created to protect balance in the realms, but they're dangerous if misused. Many before you have fallen to their allure. Now — come here. Let me heal you.”

Jessie steps toward him, thinking of the ice sword he summoned in the library.

Jessie

“Sir, I think I've tapped into a new ability with my Arcane Ring. I created a sword… made of pure ice.”

A glimmer of pride flashes across Sir Mular's eyes as the healing finishes.

Sir Mular

“An ice sword is no ordinary feat. You're connecting with the ring, Jessie — truly bonding with it. But remember, the power isn't yours to take. It's yours to protect.”

Jessie

“How do I keep control, then?”

Sir Mular leads him to the edge of the training grounds, where a crystal-clear pool reflects the morning light.

Sir Mular

“Control requires balance. Just as water flows around obstacles, so must you let the power flow without letting it overwhelm you. Focus on the water. Sense its chill, the way it moves. Summon your ice sword, and let it become an extension of that stillness.”

Jessie breathes deep, closes his eyes, and channels his mana. The familiar cold travels down his arm and shapes itself into the icy sword — but this time he keeps his mind calm, letting the power flow without letting it consume him. When he opens his eyes, the blade is shimmering, crystalline, steadier than before.

Sir Mular, approving

“Well done. The ice sword shall be your signature move. Master it, and it will serve you well.”

Jessie smiles, a real sense of accomplishment settling in — though his questions about the stranger linger. He gathers his courage.

Jessie

“Sir… have you ever met others who wield the rings?”

Sir Mular, growing somber

“Yes. There are others. Some use their rings to protect. Others… have fallen into darkness. One wielder, in particular, is a powerful lightning user — a man cloaked in shadow who carries darkness in his heart. Avoid him, Jessie. His path is one of ruin.”

Jessie's stomach tightens.

Jessie

“What if… that was the man who attacked me at the library?”

Sir Mular, gaze hardening

“How could he have gotten past the guards? You said you saw him at the library.”

Jessie

“Yeah — and this is why I've got these bruises. He was tough. I was no match. I need to get stronger.”

Sir Mular

“Then you must remember why you wield the Arcane Ring. It's not about defeating others — it's about protecting those who can't protect themselves. Keep your purpose clear, and you'll find strength in it. More challenges may come. Don't let your determination fail. Now — let's continue. You're healed up and ready.”

As they continue, Jessie's movements grow more fluid, the ice sword closer to an extension of his will than a tool he reaches for. He feels himself growing stronger, more attuned to the ring's power — though he knows this is only the beginning, and the shadows around him are far from fading.

With a final swing, he strikes the training dummy — the ice sword slicing clean through. Sir Mular nods approvingly.

Sir Mular

“That's enough for today, Jessie. Rest.”

Jessie sheathes the blade, its coolness a reminder of the balance he has to maintain, and heads home.

Jessie, to himself

But what does he mean… when my ring wasn't meant to be?

End of Chapter One
